\subsection{Pre-processing and Data Augmentation}
All images are resized to $224\times224$ pixels, the input size of MobileNetV2, and normalised using the MobileNetV2 preprocessing function (pixel values scaled to the range $-1$ to $1$). To make the model robust and to compensate for the small dataset, the training images are randomly augmented in each epoch. Validation and test images are only pre-processed, never augmented.

\subsection{Model Architecture}
MobileNetV2~\cite{mobilenet} is built on depth-wise separable convolutions and inverted residual blocks with linear bottlenecks, which greatly reduce parameters and computation compared with traditional CNNs such as VGG16. The ImageNet-pretrained network (without its original classification head) is used as the base, and a new classification head is added.

\tablestyle
\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Model architecture}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|c|l|X|}\hline
\hdrrow \hdr{No.} & \hdr{Layer} & \hdr{Description} \\
\hline
1 & MobileNetV2 (base)     & Pre-trained on ImageNet, input $224\times224\times3$, produces feature maps \\
\hline
2 & GlobalAveragePooling2D & Converts feature maps to a single feature vector \\
\hline
3 & Dropout (0.2)          & Randomly drops 20\% of units to reduce over-fitting \\
\hline
4 & Dense (4, Softmax)     & Outputs probability for glass, metal, paper, plastic \\
\hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\subsection{Training Strategy}
Training is carried out in \textbf{two stages}.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Stage 1 -- Feature extraction:} the whole MobileNetV2 base is frozen and only the new head is trained for up to 10 epochs using the Adam optimiser (learning rate \textbf{0.001}) and categorical cross-entropy loss.
  \item \textbf{Stage 2 -- Fine-tuning:} all layers except the last 20 of the base network stay frozen; these \textbf{last 20 layers} are unfrozen and trained for 5 more epochs with a very low learning rate (\textbf{$1\times10^{-5}$}) so that the pre-trained knowledge is adjusted gently to garbage images instead of being destroyed.
\end{itemize}

Two callbacks are used. \textbf{ModelCheckpoint} saves the model with the highest validation accuracy, and \textbf{EarlyStopping} (patience $=4$, monitoring validation loss) stops training when the model stops improving and restores the best weights.
