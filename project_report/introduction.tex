\section{Introduction}
The rapid growth of population and urbanisation has produced a large increase in solid waste. Recycling can recover valuable materials and reduce pollution, but it works only when waste is separated correctly at the source. In practice, people often mix recyclable materials such as paper, plastic, glass and metal, which contaminates recycling streams and sends useful material to landfill.

Computer vision and deep learning offer a practical solution. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) can learn visual features such as texture, colour, shape and reflectance that distinguish one material from another. A camera combined with a trained CNN can therefore act as an automatic waste-sorting assistant.

This project builds such a system. It uses \textbf{transfer learning} with \textbf{MobileNetV2}, a lightweight architecture designed for efficient inference, so that the final model is accurate yet small enough to run on an ordinary laptop or be extended later to mobile and embedded devices.
